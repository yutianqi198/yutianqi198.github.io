I am Yu Tianqi, currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Communication at Nanjing University.

My online alias is “Linggan Dawang” (King of Inspiration), inspired by a demon of the same name in the classic Chinese novel Journey to the West. In the story, the character’s true form is a goldfish from Guanyin Bodhisattva’s lotus pond. I chose this name for three small reasons: first, because my surname, Yu, sounds like the Chinese word for “fish”; second, because I hope to be as fortunate as a koi; and third, in a more literal sense, because I aspire to remain energetic and spirited, sustaining a steady flow of thought and inspiration.

I am fond of anime and alternative music. I am approachable and grounded, with strong communication skills, a solid sense of empathy, and a collaborative mindset.

My current research focuses on the sociology of knowledge. I am particularly interested in how intellectuals, amid the polyphonic and often noisy public sphere of our time, might reconsider their missions and redefine the ways they engage with the public.

Over the next three years of my master’s studies, I intend to devote myself to rigorous learning and steady growth, seeking—through study and reflection—to better understand myself, others, and the society we inhabit.
